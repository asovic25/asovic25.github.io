INBOX MANAGEMENT & EMAIL TRIAGE
Virtual Assistant Work Sample | Demonstration

Purpose: Demonstrate a repeatable process for reviewing a busy business inbox, prioritising messages, identifying required action and ensuring that follow-ups are not missed.

1. Daily email triage workflow
- Review new messages and identify urgent, time-sensitive or client-impacting requests.
- Categorise messages as Action Required, Waiting for Reply, Scheduling, Information or Archive.
- Flag deadlines, meeting requests, customer issues and messages requiring the business owner's approval.
- Draft concise, professional responses using the organisation's tone and approved information.
- Create follow-up reminders for unanswered requests and confirm that important items have an owner.
- Archive completed messages only after the required action has been recorded.

2. Sample inbox prioritisation
Example message | Priority | Suggested action | Target response
Client reports unable to access account | High | Acknowledge, gather details, escalate if needed | Within 1 business hour
Supplier requests confirmation of delivery slot | Medium | Check calendar/order details and reply | Same business day
Newsletter / industry update | Low | Review only if relevant; otherwise archive | No response required
Meeting invitation for next week | Medium | Check availability and accept/propose alternative | Same business day

3. Example professional response
Subject: Re: Delivery schedule confirmation

Hello [Name],

Thank you for your message. I’m checking the proposed delivery window against the current schedule and will confirm the available slot shortly.

Kind regards,
Virtual Assistant

4. Quality checks
- Names, dates, attachments and links checked before sending.
- No confidential information shared without authorisation.
- Every open request has a clear next action and follow-up date.
- Escalations are documented and sent to the appropriate person.

Note: This is a fictional practice scenario created to demonstrate workflow and communication. It is not a record of work performed for a real client.
